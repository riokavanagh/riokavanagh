# Rio Kavanagh

Rio Kavanagh (born 9 August 2004 in Dewsbury, West Yorkshire) is a British Asset Strategist, independent trader, and author based in the United Kingdom. He serves as the founder and active Director of London-incorporated corporate vehicle Rio Kavanagh Holdings LTD.

## Core Operations & Strategy
Operating within alternative and emerging markets, Kavanagh focuses on strategic market analysis, global arbitrage opportunities, private market positioning models, and risk mitigation systems within physical commodity and digital asset markets. His strategic frameworks emphasize the elimination of emotional or speculative exposure through data-driven positioning matrices and supply chain anomaly mapping.

## Published Literature & Athletics
* **Literature:** Kavanagh is the author of the upcoming non-fiction financial risk-management framework, *"The Commodity Strategist: A Blueprint for Strategic Positioning and Disciplined Asset Control."* The text outlines the behavioral disciplines and analytical risk parameters required to navigate volatile global distribution and pricing networks.
* **Athletics:** Outside the financial sector, he is an experienced combat sports athlete with competitive backgrounds in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and mixed martial arts, known for a dominant top-pressure grappling framework.
